Guide

How to use RF Swift day to day, from the Workbench and your first toolbox to engines, platforms and advanced features.

Intermediate 2 min read Updated September 27, 2026

You have installed RF Swift and opened your first lab with the Quick start. This guide covers everything after that. It is grouped the same way as the sidebar: start with Everyday use, then read the other groups when you need them.

New here?

If words like image, container or workspace are unfamiliar, read Key ideas in 5 minutes first. Every page below is marked Beginner or Advanced so you know what to expect.

Everyday use

The pages you will come back to most: the desktop app, choosing a toolbox, getting files and radios in and out, and changing a lab after you created it.

Workbench (desktop app) BeginnerMissions, terminals, notes, findings and reports in one window Choose a toolbox BeginnerWhich image to pick for radio, Wi-Fi, RFID, cellular, cars and more Included toolsEvery tool in every image, with a filter box Files & devices BeginnerYour workspace folder, USB radios, serial readers, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi Daily workflowCreate, re-enter, record and update your labs from the terminal Change a container laterAdd devices, folders, ports or capabilities without starting over Add more softwareInstall functions, apt, recipes and Nix packages compared Assessment reportsTurn a container and its workspace into a report from the CLI

Platforms & engines

RF Swift runs the same toolboxes on Linux, macOS and Windows, with Docker, Podman, Lima or the native Nix engine. Read the page for your platform or engine when something behaves differently from the Quick start.

WindowsThe installer bundle, WSL 2, USB passthrough with usbipd-win PodmanRootless containers, no daemon, and what changes for devices Nix engine (no containers) AdvancedThe same tool sets installed natively, pinned, with rollback QubesOS AdvancedRunning RF Swift in a Qubes qube, step by step Offline / air-gapped install AdvancedExport images and install them on a machine without internet Known limitsWhat each platform and engine cannot do, and why

Advanced

Configuration, host plumbing, remote labs and automation. None of this is needed to get started.
